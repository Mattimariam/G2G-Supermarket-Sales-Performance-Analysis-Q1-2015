# G2G-Supermarket-Sales-Performance-Analysis-Q1-2015
Personal Microsoft Excel project analyzing G2G Supermarket sales across cities, branches, product lines, customer segments, months, and payment methods. The analysis identified top-performing areas, customer trends, and practical opportunities to improve sales, customer engagement, and operational performance.

Key Insights

. Naypyitaw generated the highest city revenue at $110,568.

. Branch C led overall branch performance in sales and customer volume.

. Food & Beverages was the highest-selling product line.

. January recorded the highest monthly sales at $116,292.

. Female Members contributed the largest share of revenue.

. Cash was the most-used payment method.

. Total Q1 sales reached $322,967.

Recommendations

. Strengthen inventory and staffing at Branch C.

. Expand promotions and loyalty initiatives in Naypyitaw.

. Prioritize Food & Beverages through bundles and targeted promotions.

. Develop personalized offers for Female Members.

. Encourage digital payment adoption.

. Use January's performance to guide future promotional planning.

. Apply cross-selling and upselling strategies to increase transaction value.

. Monitor branch-level KPIs regularly.

. Promote Sports & Travel and Electronic Accessories to diversify category growth.
